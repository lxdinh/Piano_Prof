================================================================
LuminaKeys Octave Flex PCB - Design Specification
================================================================
Replaces 5x rigid octave-module PCBs with ONE continuous flex
panel spanning all 5 octaves of the keyboard.

Total length:     822.5 mm  (5 octaves x 164.5 mm)
Total width:      25 mm     (suggested - tune to mounting rail)
LEDs:             60 x WS2812B-2020 (2x2mm) at real piano key positions
Decoupling:       60 x 0.1uF 0603 (one per LED)
Connectors:       1 x JST SH 4P at left end (input from controller)
                  (NO output connector - chain ends in the strip)

----------------------------------------------------------------
FLEX PCB STACKUP (2-layer)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Top to bottom:
  Coverlay        25 um polyimide overlay (protects copper)
  F.Cu            1 oz copper (35 um) - signal + power
  Polyimide       25-50 um (base substrate)
  B.Cu            1 oz copper (35 um) - GND pour + power return
  Coverlay        25 um polyimide overlay
  Stiffener       FR4 stiffener under J1 connector area (~15x20mm)
                  REQUIRED to prevent flex fatigue at solder joint

Total finished thickness: ~0.2 mm (very thin, very flexible)

----------------------------------------------------------------
POWER BUDGET & TRACE WIDTHS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Worst case current (all 60 LEDs full white):
  60 x 36 mA (WS2812B-2020 datasheet max) = 2.16 A peak
Typical (mixed colors, 30% brightness): ~0.6-1 A

VOLTAGE DROP CALCULATION (single-end feed over 822 mm):
  Trace resistance @ 1oz Cu = 0.49 mOhm/sq

  +5V trace 10mm wide x 822mm long = 82.2 squares
    R = 0.49 mOhm x 82.2 = 40.3 mOhm
    V drop @ 2A = 81 mV  (1.6% of 5V - OK)

  GND return same: 81 mV

  Total round-trip drop @ 2A peak = 162 mV (3.2% - acceptable)
  Total drop @ 1A typical         = 81 mV  (1.6% - good)

RECOMMENDATION:
  +5V on F.Cu: 10 mm wide solid trace OR copper pour along the
               whole length (use most of the available width above
               the LED row).
  GND on B.Cu: SOLID copper pour covering the entire board.
  Data line:   0.2 mm minimum trace, zigzag between LEDs as needed.

----------------------------------------------------------------
LED PHYSICAL POSITIONS (all 60 LEDs, global X from board left edge)
----------------------------------------------------------------
See octave-flex-led-positions.csv for full data.

OCTAVE 1 (notes C1..B1):    x =  11.75 to 152.75 mm
OCTAVE 2 (notes C2..B2):    x = 176.25 to 317.25 mm   (offset +164.5)
OCTAVE 3 (notes C3..B3):    x = 340.75 to 481.75 mm   (offset +329.0)
OCTAVE 4 (notes C4..B4):    x = 505.25 to 646.25 mm   (offset +493.5)
OCTAVE 5 (notes C5..B5):    x = 669.75 to 810.75 mm   (offset +658.0)

LAYOUT (y from top edge of 25mm-tall board):
  LEDs:  y = 20 mm (5mm clearance from bottom edge, places LEDs as close
                   to the keys as possible when the strip is mounted
                   above the keyboard)
  Caps:  y = 14 mm (6mm above LEDs)
  J1:    y = 8 mm  (top area, no collision with cap row)

Each LED's center is aligned to its corresponding piano key center
per DIN 8996 (164.5mm octave width, 23.5mm white-key pitch).

----------------------------------------------------------------
NET LIST
----------------------------------------------------------------
POWER:
  +5V       J1 pin 1 -> all 60 LED VDD pins, all 60 cap top pads
  GND       J1 pin 2 -> all 60 LED GND pins, all 60 cap bottom pads

DATA:
  DIN_IN    J1 pin 3 -> LED1 DIN
  LED1_DOUT -> LED2 DIN
  LED2_DOUT -> LED3 DIN
  ... (chain continues)
  LED59_DOUT -> LED60 DIN
  LED60_DOUT -> NC (or left as test pad)

OPTIONAL PASSTHROUGH:
  PASS      J1 pin 4 -> NC (omit if using 3-pin connector instead)

----------------------------------------------------------------
CONNECTOR
----------------------------------------------------------------
J1 (left edge, INPUT)  -- JST SH 4P SMD horizontal
  Pin 1   +5V
  Pin 2   GND
  Pin 3   DIN_IN
  Pin 4   PASS (optional - leave NC for 3-pin reduction)

IMPORTANT: J1 must sit on a STIFFENER (FR4 backing piece glued
under the flex). SMD connectors on bare flex will rip off the
first time you plug in a cable. Spec the stiffener in fab CAM
notes: "FR4 stiffener 0.5-1mm thick, 15x20mm, centered under J1
footprint on B.Cu side".

----------------------------------------------------------------
DECOUPLING (datasheet-required, one per LED)
----------------------------------------------------------------
C1..C60   0.1uF X7R 0603, +5V to GND, within 3mm of each LED's VDD pin.
          Place each Cn next to its corresponding LEDn.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FLEX-SPECIFIC LAYOUT RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. NO COPPER POURS IN BEND AREAS. Flex fatigue happens where
   copper is wide and stiff. The whole strip is theoretically
   bendable, but the LED zones must stay relatively rigid (the
   coverlay + soldered components add stiffness there anyway).
   Use cross-hatch copper fill (50% fill, 0.5mm hatch) for the
   GND plane to allow flex.

2. TEARDROPS on all pad-to-trace junctions. Flex movement
   concentrates stress at sharp transitions; teardrops distribute
   it. KiCad: Tools > Generate Teardrops.

3. CURVED TRACES, not 90-degree corners. Use 45-degree minimum
   transitions or arc segments. Sharp corners crack under flex.

4. ANCHOR PADS for SMT components: extend pad-side copper slightly
   under the component body where possible, OR add small "anchor"
   vias/pads to mechanically lock the component to the substrate.

5. NO VIAS IN BEND ZONES if you can avoid them. Vias crack first.
   If unavoidable, use larger via pads (0.6mm pad, 0.3mm drill) for
   strength.

6. STIFFENERS under all connectors. Already noted for J1 - add a
   stiffener spec in the CAM/Gerber notes.

7. EDGE.CUTS: define the outline carefully. Flex fabs sometimes
   use a router or laser. Confirm with your fab what minimum
   inside-corner radius they support (typically 0.5-1 mm).

----------------------------------------------------------------
MANUFACTURING NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
Suitable fabs (at length 822.5 mm):
  - PCBWay flex service (supports up to ~750mm standard, 1m custom)
  - Multi-CB (Germany, supports 1m+)
  - PCBCart
  - JLCPCB does NOT support 822mm flex (caps at ~350-500mm)

Order spec to send to fab:
  Layers:           2 (F.Cu, B.Cu)
  Material:         Polyimide flex
  Substrate:        0.1mm (4 mil) polyimide
  Copper:           1 oz (35 um) both sides
  Coverlay:         0.025mm polyimide both sides
  Stiffener:        FR4 0.5-1mm, 15x20mm, under J1
  Surface finish:   ENIG (best for flex - gold doesn't crack)
                    OR HASL-LF if cost-sensitive
  Min trace/space:  0.15 mm / 0.15 mm
  Min via:          0.6mm pad / 0.3mm drill
  Outline:          As drawn in Edge.Cuts (laser cut)
  Quantity:         1-5 prototype, then 10+ for production

Expected cost: $80-200 per board at qty 1-5 (specialty flex is
expensive). Volume drops the per-piece price significantly.

----------------------------------------------------------------
ASSEMBLY NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
SMT assembly on flex is tricky. Options:

OPTION A: Order with SMT assembly service from the same fab
  Best for production. Most flex fabs offer assembly. ~$2-5/LED
  hand-placed or ~$0.50-1/LED machine-placed.

OPTION B: Hand solder
  Possible but tedious for 60 LEDs + 60 caps. Use a hot air station,
  solder paste, stencil. The flex must be held FLAT during reflow
  (clamp on a rigid temporary backer).

OPTION C: LED strip vendor partnership
  Some vendors (e.g., AdaFruit DotStar/NeoPixel makers) take custom
  PCB designs and assemble. Search "custom WS2812 strip assembly".

----------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALLATION
----------------------------------------------------------------
The 822mm strip will droop and not stay flat by itself. You need:

  - Adhesive backing (3M VHB tape, 8-10mm wide, on the back coverlay)
    OR
  - Mounting in an aluminum/PVC channel (LED strip channels exist
    in 1m lengths, typically 17mm wide x 7mm tall)
    OR
  - Custom 3D-printed bracket that snaps the flex into position
    above the keys

Recommend channel-mount: looks professional, protects the LEDs,
diffuser available, easy to remove/replace.
